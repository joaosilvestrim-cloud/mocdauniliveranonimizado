DRA Module Scope, living documentation

The scope as applied in the system: flowcharts, golden rules, the practical journey and the technical specification. One page for the business and for the developer.

What the DRA module is
The DRA is the plant investment portfolio: the analyst monthly spreadsheet turned into a system. A robot extracts ERP data, the system compares it with the last human validation, highlights what changed, allows the manual adjustments that are business decisions (status, type, CPX/IO, values, replanning, ceiling) and publishes to Power BI only what was validated, actuals flow automatically. Everything in k€, with monthly history and full traceability.
Module boundary: the DRA handles the project money (budget, CPX/IO, spend, committed and ceiling). The project itself (steps, documents, schedule, milestones) belongs to Module 1 · PSR and to the Lifecycle. This page describes the DRA only.
For the businessclient · how it works
1 · Data architecture · 3 entries: robot (GRR3/KOB1), manual entry (KO03) and upload (multi-site)
━━ actuals reach BI automatically  ·  ╌╌ replanning only after the analyst validation
GRR3: budget, actuals, committed and available per project · KOB1: current spend · KO03: order master data (entry/start/end dates, investment reason, status 30/40/50), entered manually in the system, not part of the robot.
2 · The analyst monthly cycle · monitoring with human decision, not blind automation
Accepted differences that change the current month are compensated in the following month, preserving the quarter · the initial retroactive load imports past actuals for verification.
3 · Project approval workflow · investment portal + AI authorization
The analyst records CPX, IO and AI in the system as they are issued (manual edit ✎ in the DRA Grid).
4 · DRA golden rules
1
The annual ceiling is untouchable

The yearly forecast total (green row) must not be exceeded. It is the first number the board looks at. The system monitors it with the ceiling bar; only directors change it (✎, audited).

2
Quarter volumetry

When moving values between months, the quarter total must hold. The edit dialog recalculates the quarter live and warns when volumetry would change.

3
Humans decide, the system monitors

Replanning has no fixed rule: it depends on leaders, finance and the board. The system compares and highlights; the analyst decides (Accept ERP × Keep mine). Everything audited.

4
BI in two speeds

Actuals reach Power BI automatically, without approval. Replanning only enters BI after validation (Publish to BI).

5 · Module map and the journey in practice
The screens appear in the side menu in the order of the project journey: the Overview is the entry panel and, below it, the operating path from funnel (Pipeline) to record (History).

9 · Robot and permissions · for the developer
Robot (RPA)
  • Scheduled monthly + on demand (↻ button); extracts GRR3 and KOB1 only
  • Output: standardized file, one row per IO × month, written to the official repository
  • On failure: keeps the last valid base and shows an out-of-date sync stamp
  • Idempotent: re-running a month upserts by IO × month, never duplicates
Permissions per site
  • Admin: everything on their site + robot trigger + annual ceiling
  • Editor: grid edits and spreadsheet upload for their site
  • Viewer: read and export only (write controls hidden)
  • Every write is audited (author, date, before/after); structural changes also feed the variation log

PSR Module Scope, living documentation

The Module 1 scope as applied in the system: flowcharts, golden rules, practical journey and the technical specification.

What the PSR module is
The PSR (Project Status Report) manages the engineering project itself, following the EEM cycle: registration and A–E classification, 7 steps with documentation gates (progress is blocked until the required documents are complete), evidence, baseline schedule and a calculated on track × off track status. It replaces spreadsheets and scattered folders with one auditable flow.
Module boundary: the PSR handles the project (steps, documents, evidence, schedule and status). The project money belongs to Module 2 · DRA. This page describes the PSR only.
For the businessclient · how it works

4 · PSR golden rules
1
The gate blocks progress

A step only advances with 100% of required documents. The system checks the checklist and lists what is missing; no skipping.

2
Classification defines rigor

Six criteria score the project (0–100) and define Type A–E: A/B with 57 documents, C 37, D 36 and E 16 (no Step 3). Bigger project, stricter checklist.

3
Status is calculated, not declared

On track × off track comes from the automatic comparison of actual × planned. Nobody chooses their own status.

4
Everything auditable

Every registration, document, evidence and step advance is recorded with author and date. The Auditor reads everything, writes nothing.

Module 3 Scope · Lifecycle, living documentation

Managerial monitoring by milestones, rescheduling with mandatory justification and calculated status. One page for the business and for the developer.

What Module 3 is
The Lifecycle is the managerial monitoring of progress for every project of the plant. The view is by milestones (dated deliverables, no micro-tasks) and monthly activities, against a frozen baseline: any rescheduling requires a mandatory justification and stays marked in red without erasing the original plan. All plant projects pass through here: PSR projects enter automatically (mandatory) and no-CAPEX projects (digitalization, automation) are registered directly in a simplified flow.
Module boundary: the Lifecycle handles managerial progress (phases, milestones, activities, deviations and alerts). Documents and steps belong to Module 1 · PSR; money belongs to Module 2 · DRA. This module reads both, it never edits them.
For the businessclient · how it works
How a project enters the Lifecycle
EntryWhenHow it works
Automatic (mandatory)Every Module 1 · PSR projectEnters with no user action; step documents act as milestones and feed project health; opens in the same project workspace
Direct registration (no CAPEX)Simple projects: digitalization, automation, improvements"+ New project" button: no A–E classification and no documentation gate, with a saving type (Hard Save · Cost Avoidance · Virtual Saving) and estimated saving; opens straight in the project workspace
Central rule: if this is the plant project management tool, every project passes through the Lifecycle, with or without CAPEX. The module acts as an auditor of progress.
1 · The 5-phase journey
The Lifecycle screen shows the five phases of each project with live status, project health and the evolution history. In no-CAPEX projects, Execution becomes Implementation, with no steps and no documentation gate.
2 · Rescheduling with justification · the red mark against the baseline
3 · Lifecycle golden rules
1
Milestones, not micro-tasks

The managerial level is the milestone (dated deliverable). Operational detail lives in monthly activities; this is not a task-management clone. Milestones are configurable by the project owner; start and closure are mandatory.

2
Mandatory justification

No deadline moves without a justification. The system blocks the rescheduling without it and records everything in the audit trail.

3
The baseline never disappears

The original plan is frozen; the deviation shows in red on the timeline. The board always sees agreed × actual.

4
Calculated status + alerts

On track × off track comes from actual × planned deviation, and automatic alerts notify the owner. Nobody declares their own status.
